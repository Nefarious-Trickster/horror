# THE TALL ONE: Story Bible

A first-person horror game set in a private biocontainment facility. This document is the plan everything else is built from: the setting, cast, secret, chapter structure, endings, and the systems that track your choices. Prologue and epilogue bookend five substantial chapters.

---

## Setting

**Halversen Biocontainment, Site 41.** A privately funded research facility built into a hillside, reached by one access road. Officially it studies "anomalous biological material". In practice it holds things that should not exist and studies how to use them.

- **Wing C** is the clinical heart of the site: white, bright, sterile, expensive, and quiet except for air handlers. This is where the specimens are kept.
- **Wing D** is a build-out abandoned halfway through construction, with exposed studs, plastic sheeting and work lights on extension cords. It sits directly above the old mine workings where the specimens were found. Nobody is supposed to be in there.

The site is staffed but thin at night. A security lockdown seals every exit automatically during a containment breach, and the external response team is about forty minutes out.

## Cast

| Who | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Dr. Avery Rook** (you) | Behavioural researcher on the C-14 rotation. Quiet, careful, good with the specimens: the only person who can read them. Your face and gender are never shown. |
| **Dr. Imani Okafor** | Director of Wing C, your supervisor. She speaks to you over the radio for almost the entire game: calm, precise, reassuring. She knows far more than she says. |
| **Dr. Teodor Lind** | Former lead on C-14. "Transferred off-site" eight months ago. His name is still on half the equipment labels. |
| **Sgt. Pell Halloran** | Night security lead. Trapped below Wing D during the breach. |
| **C-14A** | The first specimen. Recovered from the mine in 2019. Tall, gaunt, photophobic. Patient. |
| **C-14B** | The second specimen. Appeared in containment eight months ago, officially "a second individual recovered from the same site". Slightly smaller, restless, and it watches the staff in a way A never does. |

## The secret

Project **LANTERN** was never about containment. Halversen wanted to understand how C-14A came to exist, so they could make more.

A secretes a compound in its saliva that, given time and total darkness, rewrites a human body into something like itself. Eight months ago Okafor authorised an off-the-books exposure trial. Lind volunteered: the funding was going to be cut, and he believed he could document the transition from the inside and be reversed with an experimental counter-agent, **LANTERN-0**. The counter-agent failed.

**C-14B is Teodor Lind.**

Some of him is still in there. That is why B breaks out of containment toward Wing D: Lind's old project office is there, and the original 2019 containment chamber in the mine below it, where he hid the only remaining dose of LANTERN-0. B does not fully understand what it is looking for. It just knows it has to get there.

Okafor knows exactly where it is going and why. If anyone outside learns what LANTERN is, the site and everyone in it are finished. She needs you to stop B before it reaches what's down there, or to make sure nobody can tell the difference afterwards.

### Prologue: Intake (built)

You arrive for the morning rotation. You badge in, go through decontamination, and enter the Wing C lab, where C-14A and C-14B stand behind observation glass in their cells.

Okafor talks you through the routine. You log in, read the overnight notes, observe both specimens, and run the daily response battery: light, tone, strobe. The tests establish the core mechanic: **high-intensity strobe light drives the specimens back**. During the strobe test B reacts violently. It hurls itself at the glass and cracks it. A does nothing at all, which is somehow worse.

Okafor sends you to the store room for a sealant kit. The power fails. Alarms. When the emergency lighting comes up, both cells are open.

On the CCTV you glimpse B crawling into the dark toward Wing D. A is in the lab with you. You take the **halide lamp**, a handheld strobe, from the store room, and herd A back into Cell A one flash at a time, then seal it. Then comes a crash from the far end of the lab: the Wing D door is smashed off its frame, and red light shows in the corridor beyond.

Okafor, calm again: lockdown has sealed the site, the response team is forty minutes out, and you are the only person cleared and qualified to go in after it. *"Bring it back. If you can't bring it back, the lamp has a setting for that."*

### Chapter 1: The Build-Out

The unfinished wing: concrete floors, steel studs, sheeting that moves in the draught, construction lights on long cords, and darkness between them. **Goal:** restore temporary power to the freight lift by refuelling and starting a generator and closing three breaker panels across the floor.

- **Introduces:** the creature hunting freely; hiding behind and under things; noise (running, knocking things over) drawing it; the lamp's battery economy (strobe charge recovers slowly; spare cells are scarce).
- **Story:** you find Lind's site office. His desk calendar has the last eight months crossed out. One recorder log: *"If this works, I'll be the first person to come back."*
- **Set piece:** the generator coughs to life and every work light on the floor flickers on at once. B is standing in the middle of the floor, facing away from you. It slowly turns its head all the way round.

### Chapter 2: Pump Level

The service sublevel is knee-deep in water from a burst main. Every step makes noise; the water hides the floor and anything under it. **Goal:** bring three pump stations online in order to drain the level and open the stair to the archive.

- **Introduces:** water as a noise and vision hazard; B moving through water (you see the wake, not the body); pressure valves and timing puzzles.
- **Choice: Halloran.** You find Sgt. Halloran pinned under a collapsed cable tray with B nearby. Freeing him takes time and noise. He can be **saved** (he later relays what he saw in the mine: "It was putting things in rows. Like it was trying to remember an office.") or **left** (Okafor: *"You made the right call."* Remember that she said that).
- **Story:** maintenance logs show deliveries to Wing D months after construction officially stopped.

### Chapter 3: The Archive

A records vault and the old tiered observation theatre where C-14A was first studied. Paper, film reels, slide projectors, a dead microfiche reader you can bring back to life. **Goal:** find the access codes to the mine elevator.

- **The truth:** the LANTERN files, Lind's consent form, the trial footage. B is Lind.
- **Okafor changes:** her radio voice gets shorter, then stops answering questions, then asks you to burn the files.
- **Choice: the files.** **Upload** them to the external relay (the truth gets out) or **burn** them as ordered.
- **First non-hostile encounter:** B finds you in the archive and does not attack. It stands at the end of the stacks and leaves something on the floor before it goes: Lind's ID badge.

### Chapter 4: The Nest

The 2019 containment chamber, deep in the old mine. B has made a nest out of lab coats, office chairs and photographs: a crude, wrong copy of Lind's office. Some of the missing staff are here.

**Goal:** find LANTERN-0, or prepare to finish it. Okafor announces a gas purge of Wing D in twenty minutes ("for everyone's safety"), which will kill you too.

- **Choice: the plan.**
  - **Lure:** set the capture lure; this leads to a recapture.
  - **Overcharge:** arm the lamp's lethal setting; this leads to a kill.
  - **LANTERN-0:** only available if you found every piece of Lind's trail. You recover the last vial.
- **Exposure:** being clawed or caught here raises your exposure level (see below).

### Chapter 5: Lights Out

The purge begins. Power shuts down section by section, and gas creeps in under emergency lights. You climb back through the central core toward the surface lift while B, now desperate, hunts you through the dark. The final confrontation is at the lift shaft. Your earlier decisions and what you hold determine what you can do and what B does.

## Endings

Evaluated at the end of Chapter 5 from the choices and the tracked values below.

| Ending | Conditions (summary) | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Recaptured** | Lure plan; B survives; files burned or unread | B is returned to containment. Okafor thanks you on the record, and you are promoted to lead on C-14. The epilogue is your first morning in Lind's old office. The lights in Cell B flicker when you look at it. |
| **The Lamp's Mercy** | Overcharge plan; B killed | You kill it in the lift shaft. If you know the truth, B speaks one word in Lind's voice before it dies. The epilogue is the burial nobody else attends. |
| **The Long Dark** | You die in Chapter 5, or B reaches the surface while you run | B escapes into the hills. The epilogue is a local news report about missing hikers, months later, with a tall shape at the edge of one photograph. |
| **Lantern** (true ending) | LANTERN-0 found; files uploaded; Halloran saved; exposure low | You give B the counter-agent. It does not become Lind again, but it remembers being him, and it lets you lead it out. The epilogue is the hearings: Okafor in handcuffs, Site 41 closed. In the empty lab, Cell A's occupant turns its head toward the camera. |
| **Replacement** (secret) | Exposure high; sided with Okafor (files burned, Halloran left) | You make it out. Okafor is waiting at the lift. Weeks later the epilogue is shot from inside Cell B, looking out through the glass at a new researcher reading your name off the label. |

## Tracked values

| Value | Raised by | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `truth` (0–10) | documents, recorder logs, footage, Lind's trail items | unlocks LANTERN-0, Lind's last word, epilogue details |
| `mercy` | sparing B when you had it stunned; saving Halloran; the lure plan | true ending, B's behaviour in Chapter 5 |
| `obedience` | following Okafor's orders (burning files, leaving Halloran) | Replacement ending, Okafor's dialogue |
| `exposure` (0–100) | being clawed or grabbed, time spent in the nest without a mask | Replacement ending, late-game visual and audio distortion |
| `halloran` | saved / left / dead | Chapter 4–5 help, epilogue |
| `files` | uploaded / burned / untouched | endings |
| `plan` | lure / overcharge / lantern | Chapter 5 confrontation |

Progress and these values are saved locally at each chapter checkpoint.

## Core mechanics (for the chapters)

- **The halide lamp.** A handheld strobe. A flash staggers a specimen and drives it back, but it drains charge, and charge recovers slowly. Spare cells are rare pickups. From Chapter 4 there's an **overcharge** setting that can kill, but it takes a full charge and leaves you defenceless.
- **Flashlight.** It's always available, and it's weak. The creature can see it.
- **Noise.** Running, splashing and knocking objects over draw the creature. Walking slowly is quiet.
- **Hiding.** Lockers, under desks, behind sheeting. The creature searches deliberately; it does not cheat.
- **Creature behaviour.** Patrol, then investigate a noise, then stalk, then chase or retreat when lit. It is deliberate: it pauses, listens and turns its head before it moves.
- **Death.** Being caught is a death. You restart from the last checkpoint. Deaths during the endgame count toward the Long Dark ending.

## Tone rules

- Sterile and bright first, then progressively dirtier, darker and closer as you go down.
- Okafor is never cartoonishly evil. She's reasonable, and that is what makes her frightening.
- The creature is never shown fully lit for long. When it is, it is doing something deliberate.
- Scares come from anticipation (sound, shadows, something out of place), not from random jump scares.
